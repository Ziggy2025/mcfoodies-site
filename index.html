The process

How does it work?

  1. 1

    Let's have a chat

    A phone call is the best way to ask and answer initial questions, and to understand your projects. Call Cathy on +64 21 948 008.

  2. 2

    You submit your files

    Send recipe and ingredient specifications for compliance calculation or composition assessment, or copy and artwork files for checking.

  3. 3

    Cathy calculates or checks in Ziggy

    Using Ziggy software, Cathy provides calculated compliance information or feedback on edits needed for composition, copy, or artwork.

  4. 4

    Your team responds in Ziggy

    You, your team, and your artwork designer access Ziggy to view feedback and re-submit updated documentation or artwork.

  5. 5

    Sign-off and done

    Cathy signs off the artwork or other aspects of the project in Ziggy — and everyone goes away happy.
